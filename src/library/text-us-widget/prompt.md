Build a React + TypeScript + Tailwind "Text-Us Widget": a floating "Text us" bubble for business websites that turns a website chat into a text conversation. The visitor leaves a name, a mobile number and a message; the widget hands it to the business (a GoHighLevel "Inbound Webhook" workflow or any endpoint) and the business replies by SMS, so the lead isn't lost when the visitor closes the tab. No extra libraries. Put the phone helpers (country list, parsing, live formatting, validation, E.164, contrast colour) in a small `phone.ts` next to the component.

Props (typed): onSend (payload => Promise<void | { id }>; payload is { name, phone in E.164, country, message, consent: true, consentText, page, timestamp }; a throw or rejection shows the error state), onSent (payload, result), onError, onStateChange, onOpenChange, businessName, businessNumber (the number replies come from, shown in the confirmation), replyTime ("We usually reply in 5 minutes"), closedHint (out-of-hours line shown instead of the reply time; sending still works), defaultCountry (ISO code, default "US"), countries (override the built-in list), consentText ({business} is filled in), consentMode ("checkbox" = required tick box, "implied" = the line sits under the button and pressing Send agrees), position ("bottom-right" | "bottom-left"), container ("window" pins to the screen, "parent" stays inside a positioned parent), openOnLoad (boolean or delay in ms), defaultValues ({ name, phone, message }), team (faces in the header: { name, src? }[]), labels (partial overrides for every visible and announced string), accent (hex, default "#C6FF3D"), size ("sm" | "md" | "lg"), maxLength (default 320), draftKey (sessionStorage key, false turns drafts off), disabled, previewState ("closed" | "open" | "filling" | "invalid" | "sending" | "sent" | "error" | "disabled" | "offline", drawn still with sample data, rendering spans instead of buttons and inputs, inert) and className.

Behaviour:
- The pill says "Text us" with a chat icon. Pressing it opens a panel that grows out of that corner (scale and fade from the corner), and focus goes to the first empty field. The pill turns into a round close button with aria-expanded.
- The panel is a non-modal dialog: a header with the business name, team faces and a green "We usually reply in 5 minutes" line (or the amber closed-hours line), then name, a country-code select plus a tel input, a message with a character counter, the consent line and a full-width Send button.
- The number formats live as it's typed, per country ("(415) 555-0134", "07700 900123", "01712 345678"). Pasting "+44 …" switches the country. The caret stays where it was, and backspace over a space or bracket deletes the digit before it. Each country has a mobile pattern; the field is checked when the visitor leaves it and the error gives a real example for that country.
- Send checks everything (name, valid number, message, consent tick in checkbox mode). If something is missing, each field says why with aria-invalid and a linked message, and focus jumps to the first one.
- While onSend runs, fields are read-only, the button shows a spinner and "Sending…", and the form is aria-busy.
- On success the message turns into a chat bubble that lifts up and leaves through the top of the panel, then the confirmation slides in: the bubble with "Delivered to {business}", "Sent. Check your phone." and "We'll text you at +1 415 555 0134 from (415) 555-0199", plus "Wrong number? Edit" (back to the form with the message restored and the number selected) and "New message". Focus moves to the confirmation heading.
- On failure an error line appears, nothing typed is lost and the button says "Try again".
- The draft (name, number, country, message, consent) is kept in sessionStorage, so closing the panel or reloading the tab loses nothing. It is read when the panel first opens, so the server render never differs.
- Esc or the close button closes the panel and focus returns to the pill. openOnLoad opens it once without stealing focus, and not again in that tab after the visitor closed it.
- A visually hidden role="status" region announces opening, sending, sent (with the number) and errors.

Look: control-room style. Panel in var(--k-panel) with 1px var(--k-line) borders and rounded-2xl corners, fields on var(--k-bg), accent pill, Send button and sent bubble with text colour picked for contrast, errors in var(--k-err-text), closed hours in var(--k-warn-text). Every colour reads from the --k-* variables with dark fallbacks so it works on light and dark pages. Clear hover (lift and brighten), pressed (sink), focus-visible ring in var(--k-acc-text) and disabled (greyed pill, not pressable). With reduced motion the panel, the lifting bubble and the confirmation appear without animation. Works at 375 px: the panel never gets wider than the screen and scrolls inside if it's taller.
