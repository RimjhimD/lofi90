Build a React + TypeScript + Tailwind "Seismograph Chart": a live streaming line chart drawn like a seismograph. A pen on an arm at the right edge writes the line onto grid paper that scrolls left, smoothly, between readings. It can be paused and scrubbed, marks spikes in red ink, says when data has stopped, and is usable without seeing it. No extra libraries.

Props (typed): points ({ t: number; v: number }[], oldest first, controlled: the parent appends), label (chart name), windowMs (default 60000, time visible on the paper), historyMs (default 300000, how far back you can scrub; older readings are ignored), min and max (fixed scale; otherwise automatic with round steps and padding, always including the threshold), threshold, unit (e.g. "/min"), formatValue and formatTime (defaults: one decimal, local HH:MM:SS), onSpike(point), paused / defaultPaused / onPausedChange (controlled or uncontrolled), staleAfterMs (default 10000), announceEveryMs (default 30000, 0 = off), tableRows (default 10), loading, labels (partial overrides for every visible and announced string: pause, live, chips, stale with {s}, empty, loading, spike with {v} and {time}, threshold, readout, summary, announcements, hints, table caption and headings, spike notes), accent (ink, default "#C6FF3D"), spikeColor (default "#FF5D5D"), size ("sm" | "md" | "lg" = 150 / 200 / 260px paper height), previewState ("live" | "paused" | "spike" | "empty" | "stale" | "loading", draws one state without running anything) and className.

Behaviour:
- Drawing: an SVG whose width follows the container (ResizeObserver). The line is a smooth Catmull-Rom path built only when points change, in coordinates relative to the newest reading. A gap longer than staleAfterMs breaks the line. The pen sits left of a drum on the right; ink is clipped so nothing is drawn past it.
- Motion: a requestAnimationFrame loop moves the paper group (translate by real time since the newest reading), shifts the vertical grid pattern with it, extends a short tail from the newest reading to the pen, and eases the needle to the new value with a small spring and a faint tremble. It writes attributes directly, so React does not re-render each frame. The loop stops while the chart is off screen (IntersectionObserver) or the tab is hidden. With prefers-reduced-motion (read with useSyncExternalStore) there is no loop: the paper steps once per reading.
- Spikes: a reading above threshold right after one at or below it. Each gets a red tick on the paper's top edge, a dot and a label like "Spike 142 · 14:03:21". New crossings fire onSpike once and kick the needle upward; readings present at mount do not fire. The last four spikes are listed as buttons that pause and jump the crosshair to them.
- Pause: the Pause button, Space on the focused chart, or dragging the paper freezes it. While paused, drag or use arrows (Shift = 10 readings, Home/End) to move a crosshair through history; the paper scrolls just enough to keep it in view. The readout shows time and value and the big number shows that reading. "Live ▸" (filled with accent, readable text picked automatically) or Esc jumps back to now. New readings keep arriving while paused.
- Stale: no reading for more than staleAfterMs: the pen lifts (hollow tip), the arm rests level, no tail is drawn and the chip says "No data for 12s". Empty: "Waiting for the first reading". Loading: a spinner, "Loading readings…", aria-busy. Pause and table buttons are disabled when there is nothing to show.
- Accessibility: the chart box is focusable with role="img" and an aria-label summary (current value, low and high on screen, spike count) and is described by the keyboard hint. One polite live region gets pause/live messages and a summary every announceEveryMs; a second announces only new spikes. "Show as table" toggles a real table of the last readings (up to the crosshair when paused) with time, value and a spike note.

Look: control-room style. A rounded panel in var(--k-panel) with a caption, a big monospace value and a status chip (pulsing dot for live, warning colour for stale, red for spike). The paper is var(--k-bg) with faint vertical time lines every twelfth of the window and dashed value lines with small labels; the alert line is a dashed red line labelled "Alert at 100". The ink is the accent colour over a faint var(--k-text) underline so it holds on light and dark stages. The drum is a strip in var(--k-panel-2) with three rivets; the arm pivots from it to the pen tip, which glows red during a spike. Text on the paper has a halo in the panel colour. Every button has hover, pressed, focus-visible and disabled styles, and the focus ring uses var(--k-acc-text).
